Supplier Agreement

Leuora Digital Supplier Terms

1. Introduction: Welcome to Leuora Digital. By registering as a supplier, you agree to abide by the rules and regulations set forth by Leuora Digital and the Federal University of Technology Akure (FUTA) campus guidelines.

2. Platform Role: Leuora Digital acts as a marketplace connecting students (buyers) with vendors (suppliers). We facilitate payments and order routing but are not directly responsible for the quality of goods provided, though we enforce strict quality control.

3. Commissions & Pricing: Suppliers will list their base price. Leuora Digital reserves the right to add a markup to this base price to cover platform maintenance, marketing, and logistics. The final selling price will be determined and displayed by the Admin.

4. Payouts: Payments are processed via Flutterwave. Supplier earnings will be automatically settled to the bank account provided during registration, minus the agreed platform fee. A standard transport fee of ₦500 applies to all orders unless stated otherwise.

5. Fulfillment: Once an order is confirmed by the Admin via WhatsApp, the supplier must prepare the item for pickup promptly at the designated campus gate (North, South, or West Gate). Delays will negatively impact your supplier rating.

6. Rejections & Returns: If a product is consistently rejected due to poor quality, expired dates, or mismatch with description, Leuora Digital reserves the right to suspend or permanently ban the supplier.

7. Registration Fee: A one-time, non-refundable fee of ₦1,000 is required to become a verified supplier. This fee covers background checks and setup.

8. Data Privacy: Your business data, bank details, and contact information will be kept strictly confidential and will only be used for order fulfillment and payouts.
